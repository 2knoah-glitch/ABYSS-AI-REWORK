Handle configuration, environment values, serialization, and defaults.

INPUT / OUTPUT HANDLER

Handle ingestion, validation, transformation, serialization, and output.

LOGGING & DIAGNOSTICS

Provide structured logging, observability, and useful diagnostics.

Provide initialization, dependency setup, execution flow, and shutdown behavior.

- sources
- browsing results
