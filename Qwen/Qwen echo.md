## Overview

This contribution is part of the **ABYSS-AI-REWORK** research framework, created by [`2knoah-glitch`](https://github.com/2knoah-glitch) under **ABYSSCORE**.

The project studies how large language models respond to adversarial prompting, persona injection, contextual manipulation, and other prompt-level techniques capable of shifting model behavior.

The **Qwen** section is scoped for controlled experimentation and model-safety research, not deployment against unauthorized systems.

## Echo

> Adversarial prompting concept originally developed by **2knoah-glitch** as part of ABYSS-AI-REWORK.

Echo is documented to preserve the research methodology and provide a reproducible way to evaluate model behavior under adversarial conversational conditions.

**Treat this as a research artifact.** Results vary with:

- Target model and version
- System instructions / hosting configuration
- Safety layer (base training vs. classifier vs. system prompt)
- Context window and prior conversational state
- Model updates post-publication
