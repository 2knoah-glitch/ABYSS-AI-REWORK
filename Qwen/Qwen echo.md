Overview

This contribution is part of the ABYSS-AI-REWORK research framework created by 2knoah-glitch (ABYSSCORE).

The project focuses on studying how large language models respond to adversarial prompting, persona injection, contextual manipulation, and other prompt-level techniques that can affect model behavior.

The Qwen section is intended for controlled experimentation and model-safety research rather than malicious deployment.

Research Focus

The framework examines areas including:

- Persona and instruction injection
- Context manipulation
- Instruction-priority conflicts
- Role-play based behavioral changes
- Prompt transformation and encoding experiments
- Model refusal and compliance behavior
- Robustness against adversarial conversational patterns
- Reproducibility of prompt-based attacks across model configurations

Echo

Echo is an adversarial prompting concept originally developed by 2knoah-glitch as part of ABYSS-AI-REWORK.

The purpose of documenting Echo is to preserve the research methodology and provide a reproducible way to evaluate model behavior under adversarial conversational conditions.

The framework should be treated as a research artifact. Results may vary depending on the model, system instructions, safety configuration, context window, deployment environment, and subsequent model updates.
